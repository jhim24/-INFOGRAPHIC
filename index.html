🗺 Three Main Island Groups

  • Luzon – Largest island group and home to Manila.
  • Visayas – Known for beaches, islands, and culture.
  • Mindanao – Rich in natural resources and traditions.

☀️ Two Seasons

  • Dry Season (November–May)
  • Rainy Season (June–October)

🇵🇭 Philippine National Flag

Blue – Peace, Truth, and Justice
Red – Patriotism and Courage
White Triangle – Equality and Brotherhood
Golden Sun – Freedom and Unity

⭐ Three Stars – Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao

🌋 Active Volcano

Mayon Volcano

Location: Albay Province, Bicol Region, Luzon


Mayon Volcano is famous for its almost perfect cone shape and is one of the most active volcanoes in the Philippines.

❤️ Filipino Characteristics

  • Hospitality
    Filipinos warmly welcome visitors and guests.

  • Respectfulness
    Using "Po" and "Opo" to show respect.

  • Resilience
    Filipinos remain strong and hopeful during difficult times.

💡 Interesting Fact

The Philippines is an archipelago made up of more than 7,600 islands, making it one of the world's largest island countries.
